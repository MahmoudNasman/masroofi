Masroofi

An Android app for personal expense tracking: wallets, transactions, budgets, debts and installments, bills and savings goals.

  • All data is stored only on your phone.
  • Optional backup to your own Google Drive, in a folder the app creates.
  • No ads, no tracking, no data sharing with anyone.
